EMI Platform Structure

Who takes part, who works where, and how an EMI phone sale links the customer, the retailer, the marketplace and the finance company. Everything lives in one Odoo database: the marketplace and each finance company are Odoo companies with their own books; retailers and customers log in through the website.

1. Organisations and how they link

The marketplace sits in the middle: it sells on behalf of retailers, passes applications to the finance company and holds the sale proceeds until it pays the retailer.

2. People and roles

Each person gets one role in the organisation they work for. A higher role includes everything the one below it can do.

3. An application from start to finish

The colour of each step shows who moves the application on: the marketplace (violet), the finance company (green) or the customer (blue).

4. Who receives the down payment

Each finance company chooses one of three options, and each application copies it until a down payment is recorded. The same sale in all three cases: a phone at NPR 1,00,000 with 10,000 down.
